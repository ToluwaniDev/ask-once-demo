Running the live demo

  1. Open Bed 12 on a tablet, the HCSW phone on a phone, and the corridor screen on a laptop.
  2. On the tablet, tap Toilet. The phone buzzes and the corridor screen shows Bed 12.
  3. On the phone, tap Accept. The tablet tells the patient Amara is coming.
  4. Tap I'm at the bedside, then Done. The dashboard updates.
  5. Leave a request unaccepted to watch it escalate to the nurse in charge.

Demo only, with dummy data. Not for use with real patients.
